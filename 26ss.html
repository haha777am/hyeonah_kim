Led main-piece design and development for LIE 26SS — flats, pattern revisions, sampling and fit, materials and production communication, through to look building and styling.

Season Theme — Running

달리기에서 영감을 받은 도전과 자유. 네온과 톤다운 컬러를 대비시키고 러닝 소품을 룩에 매치했습니다. 2025년 9월 서울패션위크에서 공개되었습니다.

Challenge and freedom drawn from running, in an athleisure mood: neon set against muted tones, with running accessories worked into the looks. Shown at Seoul Fashion Week, September 2025.
